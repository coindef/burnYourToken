How is the band chosen?
  1. Session left and Weekly left: the smaller one is the effective left.
  2. Large: both over 50% (50% itself is not over), and more than 1 day until the weekly reset.
  3. Medium: one window is 20%–50% left. Both over 50% but the weekly reset is within 1 day is also Medium.
  4. Small: one window is under 20% left, or only a few hours until the weekly reset (under 5 hours).
  5. If two rules match, use the smaller band. Nothing left means wait for a reset before a new project.
  6. Session resets every few hours, so its countdown does not change the band. If you’re limited by Session and it resets soon, a note says to calculate again after it resets.
  7. Small is about 15–30 minutes. Medium is about an hour. Large is about 2–3 hours, sometimes across more than one sitting.
  8. The band uses the percentages only, not which plan you pay for. The same 50% left can do more on a higher plan; that gap isn’t published.
